Foundations are the core visual, technical, and tonal building blocks of our design system. They establish the fundamental rules and design tokens that drive brand consistency and ensure a cohesive user experience across all products and platforms.

### What you’ll find in this section

* **Design Tokens as Single Source of Truth:** Core attributes like color, typography, radius, and elevation are stored as design tokens. This ensures full visual alignment across design tools and engineering codebases, regardless of the technology used.
* **Visual & Spatial Language:** Rules for spacing, elevation, depth, and iconography that define the look, feel, and physical hierarchy of our interfaces.
* **Brand Voice & Content Rules:** Guidelines on copy, microcopy, and visual assets to keep our product communication clear, consistent, and accessible.
* **Cross-Theme & Accessibility Standards:** Built-in rules for contrast, color modes (Light & Dark), and visual clarity designed to satisfy accessibility standards out of the box.
