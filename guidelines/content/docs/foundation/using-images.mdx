## Principles

Namirial visual communication should support trusted digital transformation through warm, realistic, and human-centered imagery.

Images should feel credible, approachable, and relevant to real business contexts. The goal is not generic corporate stock photography, but visual storytelling that makes digital processes feel trustworthy, simple, and contemporary.

## Color and mood

Approved imagery should use a warm and bright atmosphere, with natural light and an overall welcoming tone. Cold blue-dominant treatments should be avoided.

### Do

- Prefer warm, sunlit scenes.
- Favor natural brightness over cold or artificial tones.
- Keep the overall mood positive, calm, and trustworthy.

### Don’t

- Avoid cold blue-dominant treatments.
- Avoid visual moods that feel distant, clinical, or impersonal.
