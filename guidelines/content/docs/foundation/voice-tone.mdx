## Communication Style

Our tone of voice is the way in which we write and speak, what we say and how we say it. Like a person, what we say is dictated by our principles, experiences and aspirations, how we say it is informed by our personality.

- Casual and conversational
- Friendly respectful
- Enthusiastic but also matter-of-fact
- Gender-neutral and bias-free
- Smart
- Trustworthy
- Short, but full of key words

### Examples

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Hi **Alex!** | Dear Very Precious Client… |
| Get your Trust ID **in less than 10 minutes** | Get your Trust ID **very fast**! |
| Our services give you superpowers! | Become a **hero** using our services |
| To | In order to |
| So | Therefore |
| Now | At this point in time |
| If | In the event of |
| We help customers | We provide help to customers |
| We analysed | We conducted an analysis of |
| We decided to | We made a decision to |
