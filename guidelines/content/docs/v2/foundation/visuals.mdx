# Namirial Logo

The Namirial logo is the central element of the brand's visual identity. It consists of two harmonious components: the **symbol** (trademark) and the **wordmark**.

![Namirial logo](/img/v2/visual-logo-v2.png)

## Trademark

The standalone symbol embodies the core geometric essence of the Namirial identity and can be used when the full logotype is not required or in restricted spaces.

![Namirial trademark](/img/v2/visual-trademark-v2.png)

## Construction

The spatial relationship and geometric balance between the symbol and the wordmark follow precise proportional rules:

* **Spacing:** The horizontal space between the symbol and the wordmark is defined by the width of the symbol’s outer arc.
* **Alignment:** The wordmark’s baseline, x-height, and ascenders are strictly aligned and harmonized with the geometric axes and diameters of the symbol.

![Namirial logo construction](/img/v2/visual-logo-construction-v2.png)

## Clear Space

To ensure maximum visibility, legibility, and visual impact across all applications, the logo must always be surrounded by an exclusion zone free of other graphic elements, copy, or trim edges.

The clearance unit **X** is defined by the diameter of the symbol's inner circle.

### Recommended (2X)

The standard spacing for everyday brand applications, web layouts, and marketing materials. Always use this distance, especially when co-branding or pairing with partner logos.

![Namirial recommended clear space](/img/v2/visual-logo-rec-safe-space-v2.png)

### Minimum (1X)

Reserved exclusively for space-constrained environments (e.g., dense digital interfaces, favicons, or micro-merchandise).

![Namirial minimum clear space](/img/v2/visual-logo-min-safe-space-v2.png)

## Wordmark & Custom Typography

The Namirial wordmark is derived from the **Outfit** typeface, customized with bespoke letterforms to give the brand a unique and proprietary character:

1. **Square Tittles:** The original round dots on the **i**s are replaced with distinct square dots.
2. **Sharp Terminals:** The rounded terminals on **n**, **m**, and **r** are replaced with precise, vertical/sharp cuts.
3. **Angled Cuts:** The soft terminals of the **a** are replaced with dynamic 45° cuts.
4. **Shortened Ascender:** The ascender of the **l** is shortened and aligned to the exact cap-line and tittle height of the **i**s.
5. **Tight Kerning:** Letterspacing is significantly tighter to deliver a cohesive, compact typographic block.

![Namirial wordmark customization](/img/v2/visual-logo-wordmark-v2.png)

> **Important:** The wordmark must **never** be recreated by typing in Outfit. Always use the original, approved vector files provided in the brand asset repository.

## Logo on Backgrounds

Always select the version that yields the strongest contrast and ensures optimal accessibility and visual balance:

* **Light & White Backgrounds:** Use the solid black logo.
* **Dark & Black Backgrounds:** Use the solid white/light logo.
* **Brand Colors (e.g., Primary Accent / Deep Forest Green):** Choose the contrasting black or white version depending on background luminance to maintain high legibility and contrast ratios.
