## Overview

A tooltip supplements a control that's already understandable on its own — it clarifies an icon-only button, spells out an abbreviation, or adds a hint. It appears on hover/focus of its trigger and disappears the moment that trigger loses hover/focus.

### When to use

Use a tooltip when:

- The trigger is already usable without it (an icon button, a truncated label) and the tooltip only adds a supplementary hint.
- The content is a short, single idea — a label, a shortcut, a brief clarification.

Consider another component when:

- The content needs to stay open while the user reads or interacts with it, or contains its own actions — use [Popover](/docs/components/messaging/popover).
- The information is essential to complete the task, not supplementary — put it inline instead of hiding it behind hover.
- The trigger is touch-only — hover-based tooltips are unreliable on touch devices; pair with a tap-to-toggle popover instead.

## Accessibility

#### Semantics

- Connect the tooltip to its trigger with `aria-describedby`, pointing at the tooltip's `id`.
- Use `role="tooltip"` on the container.

#### Keyboard

- A tooltip must appear on keyboard focus of its trigger, not only on mouse hover — otherwise keyboard users never see it.
- Dismiss on `Escape` and on blur; never require a click to close it.

#### Timing

- Don't rely on a tooltip alone to convey information required to complete a task — mouse-only hover and short auto-dismiss timers make it an unreliable single source of truth.

## Implementation

Positioning and show/hide timing need a small amount of JavaScript (or a floating-UI library); the markup and visuals are pure CSS.

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="namirial-theme.css">

<div class="nm-tooltip nm-tooltip--top" role="tooltip" id="tip-1">
  <span class="nm-tooltip__icon" aria-hidden="true"><!-- icon --></span>
  <span class="nm-tooltip__text">Autosaves every 30 seconds</span>
</div>
```

#### CSS classes

| Class | Values | Default |
|-------|--------|---------|
| `nm-tooltip--{position}` | top · top-start · top-end · bottom · bottom-start · bottom-end · left · left-start · left-end · right · right-start · right-end | — |
| `nm-tooltip--{intent}` | primary · secondary · accent · info · positive · negative · warning — omit for `primary` | — (`primary`) |
| `nm-tooltip__icon` | Leading icon slot, 24×24px | — |
| `nm-tooltip__text` | Text content | — |
